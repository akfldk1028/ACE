## Architectural response

roof is opened by a single longitudinal sky cut. The retained body stays broad
at the public ground and contracts continuously toward a north living ridge as
the diagonal cut rises. It is neither a cake-tier setback nor an independently
inflated floor stack.

2. one canonical `block` BaseVolume scale;

wedge plinth to a separate north bar; C27 is a single retained monolith formed
by subtraction from a broad carrier. The new boolean topology, matrix, values,
and BOOK path therefore do not revise C24.
