VERDICT: Only three entries advance for me - t04, whose graded vaulted bars are the one scheme where a single idea survives intact through plan, section and site orientation; t12, which turns a deep court block and a folded ridge into the most complete use of the parcel; and t14, whose crowned ring is the most distinctive figure and the easiest to develop. The middle of the field - t06, t02, t07, t13, t10, t15 - is competent but inert: stacked or split lumps whose feasibility scores flatter a missing idea, most of them leaving half the permitted volume unused. The bottom group fails on the same evidence, since t03, t08, t09, t11 and t05 all show membranes or trays too thin to be occupied, carried on supports that are never drawn, so their coverage figures describe roofs rather than buildings.
