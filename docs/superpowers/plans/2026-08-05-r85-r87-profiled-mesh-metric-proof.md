## Fixed-point hypothesis and failure

### Hypothesis

The original endpoint collapse examined an initial edge set. The fixed-point hypothesis was that rebuilding representative triangles and their edge set after each collapse would expose newly formed sub-threshold edges and allow the same bounded operation to converge to a canonical clean mesh.

### What r87 proved

The fixed-point loop executed as intended and produced deterministic five-threshold evidence, but it did not recover additional candidates:

- None of the seven residual `tiny_edge` cases became clean: `0/7` recovered.
- Six remain `tiny_edge` after the `5e-7m` attempt.
- The new `llm_inflate_carve_void` residual also remains `tiny_edge`.
- One separate prior residual moved from `tiny_edge` to `tiny_face`; it still fails canonical mesh revalidation.
- The aggregate funnel, two combined hard passes, one final-VLM hard pass, and selected `1/5` result did not improve.

The failure is metric/representative-placement specific, not evidence that the fixed-point traversal failed to run. The canonical gate rejects compact-coordinate edge length below `1e-5`. The repair budget independently limits physical displacement to at most `5e-7m`. Current collapse retains one endpoint and moves the other endpoint onto it, so the moved endpoint can consume the full edge length as displacement even where moving both endpoints symmetrically to their midpoint would halve each endpoint displacement.

## Endpoint-budget evidence

For the seven r87 post-repair `tiny_edge` witnesses:

- Current retained-endpoint collapse recovery: `0/7`.
- Six have minimum surviving physical edge lengths between approximately `5.05e-7m` and `8.32e-7m`. A midpoint placement would imply approximately `2.52e-7m` to `4.16e-7m` movement per endpoint, nominally inside the `5e-7m` budget.
- These six are only **potentially** within a symmetric two-endpoint displacement budget. Topology, chain displacement, canonical gate, component identity, section equivalence, and downstream authority remain unproven.
- The `llm_inflate_carve_void` residual has a minimum surviving physical edge of approximately `1.2986e-6m`. Its midpoint displacement is approximately `6.493e-7m` per endpoint, above the `5e-7m` budget. It is impossible to repair by a simple midpoint weld under the current displacement limit.

Therefore the evidence is exactly: `0/7` recoverable with current endpoint collapse, `6/7` potentially within a symmetric two-endpoint budget but unproven, and `1/7` impossible by midpoint under the unchanged budget.
