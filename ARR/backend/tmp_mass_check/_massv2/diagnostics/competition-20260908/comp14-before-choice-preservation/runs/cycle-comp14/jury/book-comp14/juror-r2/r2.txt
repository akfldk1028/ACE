RANKING: t08, t03, t15, t09, t16, t12, t18, t07, t10, t13, t11, t01, t17, t19, t02, t14, t04, t05, t06
VERDICT: Only t08 and its relatives t16 and t10 show a mass whose plan actually changes between the two cuts, and t08 does it while remaining entirely buildable, so it should advance first; t03 and t15 follow as the most disciplined site-responsive schemes, with t07's lifted bar worth advancing as a distinct idea if its span is resolved. The lower half of the set is dominated by constant extrusions whose whole idea is a plan silhouette (t05, t06, t11, t13) and by two ambitious pieces, t02 and t14, whose thin oversailing plates are not supported by anything drawn. A weakness across the set is scale: most alternatives use only 40-60% of the permitted floor area and under 30% ground coverage, so the parcel is engaged far below its stated capacity.
