## Confirmed failure boundaries

### Breadth scheduling

- Universal source: 82 page-zero geometry programs and two program-role
  carriers produce 164 recursive seed opportunities.
- r9 diagnostic evaluated 36.
- Only BaseVolume scopes `1/1`, `3/8`, and `1/2` ran; `1/4`, `1/8`, and
  `1/16` did not.
- Each genotype received one BOOK probe.
- Exact first36 hard-pass supply was at most 5 neighborhood, 15 gymnasium,
  and 18 cultural. A 20-card board was impossible before selection.

### Legal materialization

- `select_legal_field_affine_projection` exact-compiles only four affine
  poses and requires at least 99.5% of the assigned aggregate capacity.
  Dense monoliths dominate; courtyards, wings, radial forms, and bends are
  disproportionately removed.
- `append_floorwise_legal_projection` intersects the authored root with every
  horizontal floor band and shrinking legal section, then unions those bands.
  This is the first exact boundary where non-stepped cultural programs become
  stepped final solids.
- The renderer/exporter is not the cause.

### Morphology and selection

- Final meshes with four or more horizontal levels are measured as stepped,
  including levels created by the legal floor-band union.
- Target-three caps equal three for body phenotype and roof family, so all
  three cards may intentionally be selected from one stepped family.
- `near_duplicate_pair_count=0` uses silhouette distance below 0.10. That is
  a twin-box threshold, not a competition-grade gestalt threshold.
- Unique program/geometry hashes prove different payloads, not different
  architectural languages.
- The strong joint constraint solver runs only for target 10.
- Target-20 capacity validation requires four bands with two to three cards
  each. The total can only be 8 to 12, so target 20 can never pass.
