# LLM-Authored Diverse Legal MASS Design

Date: 2026-08-05 KST

## Objective

Produce one competition-review portfolio of exactly 20 MASS alternatives for
the live PNU. All 20 selected alternatives must originate from LLM-authored
typed GeometryProgram ASTs, pass the deterministic geometry, law, capacity,
parking, and program gates, preserve their authored architectural identity
through legal placement, and remain visibly diverse on the combined PNG.

The count and quality requirements are conjunctive. Reaching 20 never permits
disabling a diversity cap, cloning a legal mesh, replaying a forced stepped
stack, substituting another candidate's render, or weakening a hard gate.

## Selected approach

Use the existing progressive Full BOOK path as a hybrid agent pipeline:

1. The LLM architect interprets the competition brief and authors typed,
   executable GeometryProgram alternatives.
2. The geometry compiler lowers each AST from UnitBox through explicit
   BaseVolume Matrix4 transforms and typed CSG.
3. The legal materializer places the authored body into live floor sections
   while preserving one global plan frame and inter-floor relative pose.
4. Deterministic gates measure law, GFA/capacity, parking, program, geometry,
   and evidence integrity.
5. Failed or portfolio-incompatible candidates create typed replenishment
   deficits. The agent authors new descendants; rejected geometry is never
   converted into a generic legal stack.
6. The selector returns 20 only when count, authorship, law, and diversity
   contracts all pass.

Qatar National Library and other case studies are capability references for
continuous public space, circulation, section, void, span, and aggregation.
They are not shape templates and may not be copied as completed buildings.

## Architectural language contract

The author request must schedule multiple architectural strategies rather
than ask for undifferentiated maximum-FAR objects. The executable vocabulary
includes aggregation, combination, and base operatives such as interlock,
overlap, split, courtyard, void/notch, pinch, terrace link, lift, bridge,
wing, shear, taper, rotate, carve, and roof/section transformations supported
by the typed compiler.

Every authored candidate carries its requested language, principle, source
lineage, BaseVolume seed, Matrix4 chain, CSG operations, and resulting measured
phenotype. Labels are evidence, not authority: portfolio diversity is computed
from the final certified geometry and source signature.

The final 20 must satisfy the strict portfolio contract, including all six
BOOK scopes and the existing typed maximum counts. In particular, stepped and
visible-stepped maxima remain enforced in diagnostic and publishable paths.
At least one non-base operative principle must survive whenever the compatible
hard-pass supply contains one. Required body, roof, plan, chassis, geometry
family, and source-lineage breadth is taken from the strict target-20 contract
and may not be replaced by a count-only preview contract. Capacity bands are
measured evidence only: every selected candidate must reach utilization 0.70,
but an exact per-band quota may not author or balance the visible portfolio.

## Geometry preservation contract

Legal placement applies one global rotation and plan-axis frame to the
authored body. Each floor's target center is derived by transforming its
authored centroid offset from the source reference center. A floor may be
scaled down in that fixed pose or intersected by the typed legal CSG contract,
but it may not be independently recentered or realigned to the legal floor
centroid.

If the fixed-pose body cannot meet containment, capacity, visual-authority, or
identity requirements, the candidate fails with terminal evidence and enters
replenishment. It is not reflowed into a floorwise staircase. The persisted
`floorwise_legal_matrix_stack.pose_fit` must identify global relative-pose
preservation, and the exact visual mesh must agree with the occupied floor
authority.

## Count-preserving replenishment

The initial LLM supply is bounded and batched. Selection deficits are expressed
as typed requests for missing scope, principle, phenotype, roof, plan, chassis,
source lineage, capacity band, or compatible distance. Replenishment prioritizes
the missing cells and excludes attempted work identities and certified visual
duplicates.

The run may stop only when 20 compatible hard-pass LLM-authored candidates are
selected or when the bounded run budget is exhausted. Budget exhaustion must
report an incomplete portfolio and its exact deficits; it must never fill the
remainder by disabling typed caps.

## Agent and deterministic authority

The LLM owns design intent and typed geometry authorship. It does not decide
whether its work complies with law. PNU geometry, setbacks, height, BCR, FAR,
capacity, parking, program, mesh validity, and portfolio compatibility remain
deterministic authorities. Optional VLM critique may request typed revisions
but cannot certify law or replace geometry evidence.

LLM authorship may arrive from the paid provider runtime or from a persisted
Codex OAuth agent-authored manifest. The latter must identify its provider as
`codex_oauth_llm_geometry_author`, retain the authoring-session/request
lineage, and pass the same typed AST parser and compiler gates; it may not be
relabeled procedural output.

When LLM authorship is requested, final completion requires
`llm_authored_selected_count == selected_count == 20`. A run with zero LLM
authors, cached labels without authored AST identity, or deterministic-only
selection cannot claim this contract.

## Verification and acceptance

Focused tests must prove:

- a real authored mesh with a shifted upper floor retains a nonzero relative
  offset after legal materialization;
- an explicit target center cannot silently fall back to the host centroid;
- capacity retry preserves authored pose and can still reach legal targets
  when geometrically feasible;
- target-20 diagnostic fallback retains typed maximum counts;
- a live-author target requires 20 selected LLM-authored AST identities;
- missing diversity causes typed replenishment or an incomplete result, never
  count-only recovery.

The final live run must persist and expose:

- exactly 20 selected and 20 deterministic legal/final hard passes;
- 20 distinct LLM-authored AST lineages bound to their geometry hashes;
- six of six BOOK scopes;
- no typed diversity-cap violation;
- no floorwise-centroid reflow pose mode;
- no cloned certified mesh or visual duplicate;
- a combined 20-candidate PNG and individual candidate renders.

Acceptance requires inspecting the combined PNG, not only JSON counts. The
board must show materially different silhouettes and spatial organizations;
an all-staircase or near-all-staircase board fails even when every card passes
law and capacity. The existing `/design/language` MASS PORTFOLIO view must load
the same 20 persisted candidate identities and their stage evidence.

## Error handling and non-claims

Provider, credential, rate-limit, compilation, legal-fit, or evidence failures
remain explicit terminal outcomes. The run may reuse a hash-identical cached
LLM response only when its authored-program identity and request lineage are
persisted. It may not silently switch to deterministic authorship while
claiming live-agent completion.

This portfolio is deterministic system evidence for design review, not a
professional legal approval or a substitute for architect and authority review.
