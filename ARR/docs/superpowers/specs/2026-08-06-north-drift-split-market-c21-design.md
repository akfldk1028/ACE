`UnitBox -> canonical BAR BaseVolume -> authored Matrix4 -> continuous z taper -> split_wing(WEST, ground spine)`

## Self-review

- **No template catalogue:** the artifact is one static response for C21; no
  SPECS list, loop, family generator, fallback family, or parameter sweep is
  introduced.
- **Matrix4 meaning:** Matrix4 authors the normalized BaseVolume placement and
  continuous drift. It does not contain a completed legal pose or a floorwise
  transformation.
- **No numeric repair loop:** all architectural values are frozen before the
  canonical validator. Parser-contract correction may only repair a declared
  type/name/range mismatch before hashing; it may not react to exact-fit or
  capacity output.
- **Distinct graph:** the new terminal topology is split-wing plus ground
  spine. Reuse of the general taper verb does not make it a C17/C18 numeric
  variant because the source tree, access topology, selected path, scope,
  Matrix4, and visible relation are all different.
- **Known risk:** the split seam and local octant compression remove volume.
  Capacity and exact legal fit are therefore uncertain and remain exclusively
  the validator's measurement, not a reason to add floors or tune morphology.
