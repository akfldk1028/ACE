# Deep south-to-north public-roof replenishment

## Goal

Author one new direct Codex OAuth static AST for canonical
`neighborhood_living`. The mass is one connected public-room carrier under a
single deep oblique roof/ramp that rises continuously from south to north. It
must contain no floor-index, z-breakpoint, legal-band replay, stepped operator,
or copied legal polygon, and its only public-access relation must terminate on
the verified program-frame west side.

## Causal context

The v30 replenishment request uses the complete persisted v50 capacity-deficit
list, plus four typed legal-fit observations:

- A's `3/8` puncture carrier was whole-solid affine-fit infeasible.
- B's `1/2` pinch carrier was whole-solid affine-fit infeasible.
- C17's `1/1 taper+bend` carrier exceeded the 120-second validation budget.
- C16 was limited by the top band in all 36 inspected pose families; at the
  preliminary scale only the top band overflowed, and its continuous
  northward recession was qualitatively too shallow.

These observations constrain the next design but do not provide its plane
equation or author a legal-floor profile.

## Considered approaches

1. **Selected: one sliced roof/ramp plus a simple offered taper.** A block
   BaseVolume receives one deep south-low/north-high oblique half-space and a
   terminal west threshold. The newly rotated BOOK offer contributes one
   `1/2` long-axis taper before that protected threshold. This path does not
   add an upper volume and is cheaper than the failed bend path.
2. One roof/ramp plus the offered `3/8` skew. This preserves volume but can
   enlarge the upper bounding box, contrary to the observed top-band limit.
3. Intersect the block with a separate wedge operand. This makes the plane
   explicit but adds a boolean and avoidable exact-fit runtime.

## Frozen design

The authored source graph is one unary lineage:

`UnitBox -> block BaseVolume -> Matrix4(host aspect) -> slice(deep roof/ramp) -> notch(west threshold)`

- The Matrix4 plan ratio is derived only from generation-host dimensions
  `12.865:8.009`; it is not a parcel or legal-floor placement matrix.
- The slice is an architectural half-space with
  `normal=[0, 1, -0.72]`, `offset_ratio=0.36`, and
  `keep_side=positive`. It is one continuous surface with no floor or z
  controls.
- The terminal west notch uses `ratio=0.30`, `width_ratio=0.42`, and
  `height_ratio=0.64`. These are direct architectural choices inside the
  declared neighborhood threshold contract: depth `.28-.40`, width
  `.38-.55`, height `.58-.82`.
- The earlier proposed `.20/.30/.46` notch is discarded before validation as
  a parser-contract correction. It is not a law/capacity-driven numeric
  revision and is never packaged or validated.
- The selected newly offered path is
  `book:path:9cb42d9be8b7d3ae296513612f8869645a71f0a27938e27399f45cdd253fd6ba`,
  `1/2 / long_axis / taper(v4)`. Taper is absent from the source AST and is
  inserted before the terminal west relation.

## Validation and terminal handling

After the prompt/context evidence and static fragment are persisted, the
frozen AST is packaged and sent through the canonical v11 validator exactly
once. No parameter, path, or topology may change after validation starts. A
hard pass is promoted unchanged; a parser, compiler, exact-fit, capacity, or
runtime failure is archived unchanged with its terminal evidence.
