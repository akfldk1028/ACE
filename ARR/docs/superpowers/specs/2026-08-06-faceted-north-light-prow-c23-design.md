## Approved architectural response

room resolves into a north-light prow.  A faceted two-end envelope gives the
hall an oblique civic edge and a compact, north-biased crown without emitting
floors, steps, or a legal setback profile.  The public threshold is terminal
and explicitly bound to program-frame WEST (world-frame primary access EAST).

1. one `UnitBox` primitive;

   top endpoints;

   loft envelope; and
6. one terminal WEST access notch.

The selected offered BOOK path adds a bounded local short-axis shear on the
`1/4` scope before the protected terminal access relation.  It is not
pre-applied in the source AST.
