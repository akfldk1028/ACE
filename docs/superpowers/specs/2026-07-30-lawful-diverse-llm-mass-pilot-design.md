# Lawful Diverse LLM MASS Pilot Design

## Objective

Prove that a live LLM can author a materially diverse set of architectural
MASS solutions from one canonical UnitBox/BaseVolume language, while every
surviving solution obeys real-PNU law, capacity, floor, and parking constraints
and receives image-backed VLM review.

The pilot is not a named-family coverage exercise. Triangular, disc,
interlocking, long-span, stepped, and other forms are possible outcomes of the
same language. None is mandatory, prohibited, or assigned a quota.

## Authority and generation

The production author receives site, law, capacity, program, parking, and
generic geometry-language contracts. It emits typed `GeometryProgram` ASTs
without recipe or family IDs.

The canonical seed is `1/1 UnitBox`. Matrix4 owns affine proportion and
placement. Generic typed modifiers and composition operators own topology and
architectural relations. Existing named recipes are regression witnesses only.

Post-compile morphology labels may support filtering and collapse detection but
must never route production generation.

## Candidate and portfolio invariants

Each candidate must compile into one connected, watertight, manifold MASS.
Program, geometry, legal-floor, capacity, parking, render, and VLM evidence are
bound through exact hashes and PNU.

Portfolio diversity is evaluated from scale-invariant morphology, floor and
section organization, silhouettes, topology, and VLM critique. Hash difference
or small parameter noise is insufficient. Stepped mass is valid as one
solution, but a stepped-only or otherwise repetitive portfolio fails.

No named form is required to appear. Diversity is an outcome property, not a
family checklist.

## Legal and parking flow

The initial site is PNU `1168011800104170004` unless the user changes it.
Structured law evidence and deterministic solvers remain final authorities for
BCR, FAR, height, GFA, containment, floor capacity, and parking.

Legal projection must preserve authored identity. It may fit, clip, or reject
through typed, traceable operations, but it may not silently replace a
non-stepped design with generic stepped floor prisms.

The existing pre-legal creative command is an upstream component only. A
candidate marked `legal_review=not_evaluated` is ineligible for final
selection.

## VLM review

VLM review is mandatory before acceptance. It receives exact final-legal
renders with matching program, geometry, PNU, and legal evidence identities.
It evaluates visible architectural coherence, diversity, program fit, and
failure modes. It cannot rescue failed geometry, law, capacity, or parking
gates.

Reference retrieval and VLM submission remain separate events. Only submitted
images are recorded as used by the VLM.

## Cost boundary

The first live pilot has a hard ceiling of 20 paid provider requests across
LLM authoring, VLM review, and explicitly budgeted repair.

Use one structured batch author request when possible, cache before network
access, disable automatic retries, persist response IDs and usage, and stop at
the ceiling. The ceiling is not a spending target.

The execution plan must ensure that all candidates claimed as VLM-reviewed were
legibly submitted. Portfolio-level review may screen the full set; individual
review is required for any candidate claimed as finally accepted.

## Acceptance

The pilot is accepted only when:

- production used no recipe or family selection;
- all surviving candidates share canonical UnitBox lineage;
- all surviving candidates pass real-PNU geometry, legal, capacity, floor, and
  parking gates;
- the portfolio demonstrates material outcome diversity without form collapse;
- every finally accepted candidate has exact image-backed VLM evidence;
- paid calls remain at or below 20 with no hidden retries;
- artifacts and machine-readable memory record exact truth and limitations.
