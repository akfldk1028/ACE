VERDICT: The field divides sharply between tiles that carry one sectional argument - the courtyard-and-roof pieces t05 and t03, and the gateway pair t14/t13 - and a tail of plain extrusions (t09, t10, t01) that offer no legible idea at all and are not rescued by their buildability. The whole set shares one weakness that no tile escapes - FAR runs 41-70% against a 250% allowance and coverage never passes 31%, so every scheme is an object placed on the parcel rather than a building that has reckoned with its edges.
