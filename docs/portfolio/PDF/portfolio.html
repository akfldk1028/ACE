03 · Project B — Findings

Six Counterintuitive Findings

Finding 1 · Cost Inversion

3-agent swarm cheaper than 2-agent chain.

Swm-3: 3,203 tok   vs   RR-2: 4,759 tok
U=95, p<0.001

Finding 2 · Debate Quality Decline

Monotonic decline across rounds (Du et al. ICML 2024 contradicted).

3.8 → 3.4 → 3.3 (G-Eval Sonnet 4.5)

Finding 3 · B1 / B2 Split

Centralized vs decentralized scaling diverges.

B1: 1.48× sub-linear · B2: 3.17× super-linear
New hierarchy: A ≈ B2 < B1 ≈ C ≪ D

Finding 4 · Keyword Optimal · Negative Result

800-run controlled exp: ΔU adaptive vs keyword TERMINATE.

7/8 patterns: keyword sufficient
Refl-2 ΔU: +258% overhead · only Swm-4: −70%

Finding 5 · Cost Prediction Model

Random Forest, 5-fold CV.

R² = 0.54 · top feature: agents × max_messages
46% remains task-content (honest boundary)

Finding 6 · Difficulty Dominance

Task difficulty > pattern choice. 5 LLMs · 669 runs.

η²(difficulty) = 0.363 vs η²(pattern) = 0.039
Solo dominates · Refl-2 helps Haiku@medium d=1.26

ΔU Diagnostic Framework

ΔU(t) = ΔQ(t) − λ · ΔC(t)

All 14 patterns saturate within 1.1–2.4 turns. λ-insensitive (0.0 → 0.5 yields ≤0.1 turn difference). Use as diagnostic, not replacement.
