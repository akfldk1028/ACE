## Architectural decision

The building is one connected mixed-use commons with a west public spine and a calm, north-held lantern-like upper mass. Its authored ancestry is:

`UnitBox -> block BaseVolume -> one normalized host-aspect/plan-pose Matrix4 -> one continuous NW-anchored anisotropic taper -> terminal WEST public threshold notch`

The taper is the individual C20 LLM authorship decision. It is neither a shared recipe nor an inverse legal transform. The BOOK notch is absent from the source AST and is inserted causally by the selected path before the terminal access suffix.

## Frozen morphology

- Canonical UnitBox: `box(1, 1, 1)` followed by the canonical block scale `[1, 1, 1]`.
- Global Matrix4: generation-host aspect `12.865 / 8.009` plus a 4-degree normalized plan pose, represented as one affine matrix after BaseVolume and before body operations. It contains no parcel coordinate, legal placement, floor transform, or capacity correction.
- Continuous taper: vertical axis, start scale `[1.0, 1.0]`, end scale `[0.96, 0.30]`, NW anchor at the transformed normalized northwest corner, subdivisions `4`. The west spine remains legible while the upper commons becomes a slender north-held lantern; no floor boundary or legal-band breakpoint is encoded.
- Terminal public threshold: `notch(side="west", ratio=0.28, width_ratio=0.38, height_ratio=0.58)`. These are the declared neighborhood threshold bounds and are frozen before validation.
- Selected BOOK operation: long-axis triangular `book_notch(axis="x", corner="nw", ratio=0.4008)` from the exact production-helper offer. The source does not preapply it.
