VERDICT: The set is dominated by one repeated parti - a thin plate hovering over small blocks - which in t04, t06, t09 and t14 is drawn without the structure to carry it and with too little floor area to develop. Only t01 delivers a whole-parcel idea with solid section, edge response and developable floorplates; t07 is safe but generic, and t10 and t12 hold distinctive ideas worth carrying. t01 advances, with t07, t10 and t12 as reserves; the plate schemes should not advance until a section shows what holds the plate up.
