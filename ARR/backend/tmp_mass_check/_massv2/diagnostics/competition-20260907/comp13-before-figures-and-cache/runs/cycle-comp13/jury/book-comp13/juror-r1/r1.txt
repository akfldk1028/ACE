VERDICT: The set is dominated by extruded bars and boxes at low utilisation; only the terraced court (t08), the ring with corner pavilions (t10) and the lifted bar (t07) state an idea in section as well as plan. t08 and t10 would advance, with the bridged twin bars (t19) as a third if the bridge is developed; the tapered monoliths and the lifted star should not, the star for unsupported cantilevers and the monoliths for ignoring the parcel entirely. Several tiles are near-duplicates differing only by a rooftop fin or a lifted plate, and those tokens add nothing.
