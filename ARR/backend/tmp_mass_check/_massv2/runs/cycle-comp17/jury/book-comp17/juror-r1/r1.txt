RANKING: t05, t03, t02, t14, t13, t08, t11, t15, t06, t04, t10, t16, t07, t12, t09, t01
VERDICT: Only three schemes carry an idea that survives both plan and section - t05's warped ring around a court, t03's courtyard with opposed roof curvatures, and the bridged pair t13/t14 - and of those t05 is the only one that is at once distinctive, buildable and developable, so it and t03 should advance, with t14 as a third if a sectional void is wanted. The middle of the field (t02, t04, t06, t11, t15) is competent but each rests on a single gesture, while t09, t10, t01, t07 and t16 are extruded polygons or applied roof tilts that state nothing an architect could develop. The shared failure of the set is scale: every entry occupies 18-31% of the permitted ground and 41-70% of the permitted area at two to four of five storeys, so no scheme has yet been pushed to the size at which its idea would actually be tested.
