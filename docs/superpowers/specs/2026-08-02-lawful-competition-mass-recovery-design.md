# Lawful Competition MASS Recovery Design

## Goal

Generate materially diverse competition-grade MASS alternatives for one real
PNU and one program while preserving deterministic law, FAR, BCR, floor,
parking, geometry-identity, and final image-backed VLM authority.

## Causal Flow

```text
LLM-authored UnitBox/Matrix4/BOOK GeometryProgram
-> compile and hash deduplication
-> principal-frame whole-solid legal placement
-> deterministic law/FAR/BCR/floor/parking gates
-> developmental VLM critique
-> typed AST repair and complete deterministic revalidation
-> exact-final-render VLM acceptance
-> certified-mesh diversity selection
```

Production candidates are authored by the LLM. Named buildings, architects,
fixed form recipes, parcel-coordinate vertex recipes, and final morphology
quotas are prohibited. Deterministic programs remain fixtures only.

## Authority Boundaries

- Law and numeric solvers own compliance.
- The LLM owns architectural AST authorship and typed repair.
- Matrix4 owns common principal-frame placement.
- Polygon/CSG owns exact legal intersection, not automatic floor-plate
  reconstruction of a non-stepped authored body.
- Base VLM supplies critique and graph edits; it cannot delete a parent.
- Final VLM owns visual acceptance of the exact final legal render.
- The selector owns portfolio compatibility without weakening any candidate
  hard gate.

## Progressive Acceptance

The only promotion sequence is target 3, target 10, then target 20. Each stage
is a complete reduced portfolio run. Pre-legal boards and no-VLM runs are
diagnostics, never acceptance.

| Target | Raw AST | Compile limit | VLM requests | Runtime |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 36 | 24 | 4 | 45 min |
| 10 | 90 | 70 | 13 | 90 min |
| 20 | 160 | 120 | 24 | 180 min |

Every selected identity must have unique program, geometry, and visual hashes.
Certified final meshes are clustered without named-form labels; no cluster may
contain more than `ceil(target * 0.25)` selected candidates.

## Failure Evidence

An infeasible strict portfolio remains failed. It must also emit a separate
witness board containing deterministic hard-pass candidates, rejected
candidates, and typed failure reasons. A witness is never publishable.
