VERDICT: The set divides sharply between masses that are actually built and masses that are only implied - the leading group (t12, t13, t06, t04) carries a legible idea in volumes that bear to ground and uses a fair share of the 250% envelope, while the bottom group (t03, t08, t01, t11) spends its ambition on thin plates hovering over a few small pads, delivering 26-119% FAR with no developable structure beneath. I would advance t12 and t13 outright, t12 for the light court that makes a deep dense block habitable and t13 for the shift-and-stack that turns density into section, holding t06 and t04 as reserves for their disciplined terracing and for the only convincing rhythm of separate volumes in the field. Most of the remainder are single-move extrusions whose ideas would not survive development at all.
