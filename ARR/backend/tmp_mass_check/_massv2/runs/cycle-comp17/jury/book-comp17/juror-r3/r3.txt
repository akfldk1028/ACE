RANKING: t15, t05, t14, t03, t13, t02, t11, t10, t08, t04, t09, t12, t07, t16, t06, t01
VERDICT: Only t15 and t05 combine a single legible idea with a section that survives development, and I would advance both, with t14 as the third seat because its plan pair actually evidences its ground-split-and-bridge proposition rather than merely asserting it. The curved-roof group (t03, t04, t06, t08) carries the set's most interesting solid-void thinking but repeats itself, and only t03 earns its bulk; t06 needs re-examination because its ground plan and its perspective disagree about how the mass meets grade. The cruciform pair t07 and t16 and the stacked t01 are the clearest failures, all three depending on planes or overhangs that taper to nothing, and the whole field sits far under the FAR envelope, none above 70% and two below 45%.
