RANKING: t08, t16, t15, t03, t01, t07, t09, t06, t19, t14, t04, t11, t13, t10, t12, t18, t02, t05, t17
VERDICT: The set divides into three groups - terraced court schemes (t08, t16) that carry one idea through mass, plan and section without asking for undrawn structure; competent bar compositions (t15, t03, t01, t07, t09, t06) that are buildable but mostly restate a single move; and a tail of plain extrusions (t11, t13, t05) and pinwheel bridges (t12, t18, t02, t14) whose boldness rests on overhangs and separated pieces the drawings do not resolve. I would advance t08 and t16 outright, and t15, t03 and t07 for development - t07 in particular because its lifted bar is the only scheme that makes the ground itself the subject, provided the span and its two supports are demonstrated. t02, t04 and t14 hold the most memorable figures in the set but are held back by them, since each depends on a transfer or a floating piece that is asserted rather than shown.
