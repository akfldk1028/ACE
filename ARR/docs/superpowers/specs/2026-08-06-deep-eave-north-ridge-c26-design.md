## Root-cause response

C19 and C20 were limited by their upper outer envelope.  C21, C22, and C23
also lost too much useful lower/full-band area because their full-height
taper, lift, split, or loft relation reduced the carrier from the ground.
C26 therefore changes topology rather than adjusting those parameters: a
prismatic market room remains broad below one architectural eave, while a
continuous faceted roof rises to a slender north-biased ridge.

The eave is an architectural wall-to-roof joint.  It is not a legal floor,
setback band, GFA plate, or copied normalized legal-section boundary.  The
roof is a single connected lofted envelope; no independent floor solid or
stair-step mass exists.
