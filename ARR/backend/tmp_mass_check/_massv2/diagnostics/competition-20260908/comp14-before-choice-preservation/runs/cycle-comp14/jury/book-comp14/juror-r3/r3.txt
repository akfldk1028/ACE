VERDICT: Only a handful of these propose anything in section - t07 lifts a bar clear of the ground, t10 dissolves a courtyard ring into pavilions above, and t19 joins above what it separates below - and those three are what I would advance. A second group (t01, t02, t04, t14) is formally ambitious but leaves its boldest junctions, cantilevers and near-touching pieces unevidenced, and would have to show how they stand before being taken further. The remainder are extruded footprints of varying outline complexity; t15 and t16 in particular add serration at a size too small to build or develop, and t05, t11, t13 and t17 offer no architectural idea beyond a shape on a plot.
